DPP deadlines: separate binding dates from indicative policy timelines.

Not every product has the same DPP deadline. This timeline separates confirmed implementation milestones from indicative ESPR working-plan dates.

DateMilestoneStatus
20 Jul 2026EU DPP Registry became operationalCompleted
Sep 2026Commission roadmap lists decision work on remaining DPP standardsRoadmap milestone
Q4 2026Sector-specific iron & steel work and battery access-rights workIndicative / pending
18 Feb 2027Battery passport for LMT, EV and industrial batteries above 2 kWhBinding date
2027Indicative ESPR measure timing for textiles, tyres and aluminiumWorking-plan timing
2028Indicative ESPR measure timing for furnitureWorking-plan timing
2029Indicative ESPR timing for mattresses and ICT productsWorking-plan timing
Do not treat indicative adoption dates as application deadlines. Binding obligations and transition periods come from final product-specific legal acts.

Battery Passport is the clearest near-term date

Article 77 of the EU Battery Regulation sets 18 February 2027 for LMT batteries, EV batteries and industrial batteries above 2 kWh.
