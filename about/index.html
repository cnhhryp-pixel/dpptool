About

Practical DPP tools built around verifiable requirements.

DPPTool is designed to help companies structure product data, screen DPP scope and prepare for EU Digital Product Passport requirements without turning regulatory uncertainty into false deadlines.

Our approach

We separate confirmed legal requirements from working-plan priorities and proposals. Core legal statements link back to primary EU sources wherever practical.

What DPPTool is not

DPPTool is not a law firm, notified body, market-surveillance authority or EU institution. The tools are informational and do not constitute legal advice or certification.

Product roadmap

  • Free product-scope and readiness tools
  • Structured DPP data generation
  • QR / identifier validation
  • Sector templates and supplier-data workflows
  • Machine-readable exports and API-ready schemas

Why DPPTool exists

Digital Product Passport implementation sits between regulation, product master data, supplier evidence, identifiers, APIs and physical data carriers. DPPTool focuses on turning those topics into practical browser tools and implementation guides that teams can use before committing to a software platform.

Editorial principles

  • Separate binding legal requirements from indicative working-plan timelines
  • Link important regulatory claims to primary EU sources where practical
  • Do not describe a QR code or PDF as a complete DPP by itself
  • Keep free tools browser-based wherever possible
  • Mark roadmap functionality clearly instead of pretending it is available

Who the site is for

The resources are designed for manufacturers, EU importers, brands, non-EU exporters, compliance teams, product-data teams and developers preparing DPP workflows.
