How the Supplier Template Generator works

Select the fields you want to request from suppliers. The tool builds a CSV header and sample row locally in your browser, then lets you download the template.

What each field is for

FieldPurpose
supplier_idStable machine-readable supplier reference
component_idLinks evidence to the exact material or component
materialCaptures material or composition information
country_of_originSupports origin and sourcing records where relevant
recycled_contentPrepares for product rules that use recycled-content data
compliance_docsLinks declarations, tests and supporting documents
evidence_urlProvides a consistent reference to source evidence

Supplier collection workflow

  1. Define each field before sending the template.
  2. Ask suppliers to use stable IDs.
  3. Validate units, formats and accepted values.
  4. Link evidence to a product or component version.
  5. Keep a review queue for conflicts and missing data.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Supplier Template Generator create?

It creates a simple CSV header using the supplier-data categories you select and lets you download the result.

Is this a legally complete DPP supplier template?

No. It is a generic starting point. Required supplier fields differ by product group and applicable legal measure.

Why use supplier IDs instead of supplier names only?

Stable supplier IDs reduce ambiguity when company names, spellings or trading names change and make data easier to link across systems.
