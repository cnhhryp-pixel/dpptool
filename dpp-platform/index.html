Platform architecture

What should a serious DPP platform actually do?

A useful DPP platform is a product-data and compliance system. QR generation is only one small part of the workflow.

Seven platform layers

Product master

Stable product records, identifiers and versions.

Supplier data

Structured upstream evidence and field ownership.

Compliance records

Declarations, tests and technical files linked to products.

Passport publishing

Public and restricted product information with data carriers.

Registry

Registration workflows for products in scope.

API & exports

Portable structured data for ERP, PIM and external systems.

Lifecycle

Versioning, maintenance and long-term availability.

DPPTool's product direction

The current site provides free browser tools and implementation resources. Hosted passports, bulk imports, supplier workflows and enterprise integrations remain roadmap features until they are genuinely available.
