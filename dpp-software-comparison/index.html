Buyer checklist

How to compare DPP software without getting distracted by QR demos.

A serious DPP platform should be compared on data portability, legal adaptability and operational workflow — not just landing-page design.

AreaQuestions to ask
Data portabilityCan you export all passport data in machine-readable formats without vendor lock-in?
IdentifiersCan the system support model, batch or item-level passports and persistent identifiers?
RegistryCan it support EU DPP Registry registration and API-based workflows where applicable?
Supplier dataCan suppliers submit structured data with validation and version control?
Access rightsCan public, professional and authority-level information be separated?
IntegrationsDoes it support CSV, API, ERP/PIM/PLM and webhooks?
LifecycleHow are updates, version history, continuity and service-provider exit handled?
Sector adaptabilityCan schemas change as delegated acts and technical standards evolve?

Red flags

  • The vendor equates a QR landing page with full DPP compliance
  • No full-data export or unclear exit process
  • One fixed schema presented as universal for every product category
  • No explanation of access rights or lifecycle maintenance
