Frequently asked questions

Does this QR generator create a compliant Digital Product Passport?

No. It creates a QR symbol for a URL. DPP compliance also depends on identifiers, required data, access rules, registration and product-specific legislation.

Can a QR code be used as a DPP data carrier?

The European Commission identifies QR codes as one example of a data carrier that can provide access to DPP information, subject to the applicable product rules.

What URL should I encode?

Use a stable passport or resolver URL tied to a persistent product identifier rather than a temporary marketing campaign URL.
