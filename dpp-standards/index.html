Technical standards

DPP standards are turning the legal framework into an interoperable system.

The Commission reported in July 2026 that six of eight harmonised DPP standards were already available, covering core technical building blocks such as identifiers, data carriers, APIs and storage.

Core technical areas

Identifiers

Unique product, operator and facility identifiers.

Data carriers

Machine-readable links between the product and passport.

APIs & exchange

Interoperable data exchange between systems and service providers.

Storage

Availability, portability and continuity of passport data.

Access rights

Rules for public, professional and authority-level data.

Interoperability

Open technical design that avoids vendor lock-in.

Why standards matter to buyers

A DPP platform should be judged by how easily data can be exported, resolved, shared and moved — not only by how attractive the QR landing page looks.
