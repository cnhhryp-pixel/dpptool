Regulation (EU) 2024/1781

ESPR is the framework. DPP is one of its key mechanisms.

The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation enables product-specific ecodesign and information requirements across a broad range of physical goods placed on the EU market.

ESPR vs DPP

TermMeaningPractical role
ESPREcodesign for Sustainable Products RegulationLegal framework for product sustainability and information requirements.
DPPDigital Product PassportStructured product-specific data accessible through a data carrier.

2025–2030 priority areas

The Commission working plan prioritises iron and steel, aluminium, textiles/apparel, furniture, tyres and mattresses, alongside energy-related products and horizontal work such as repairability and recycled content for electrical and electronic equipment.

Planning signal, not a universal deadline: inclusion in a working plan does not by itself mean every product in that category already has a mandatory DPP.

Why exporters should prepare early

Non-EU manufacturers may need to provide structured product and supply-chain data to EU customers and importers. The lowest-cost preparation step is to standardise identifiers, materials, supplier evidence and compliance documents before a product-specific measure becomes applicable.
