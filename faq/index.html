Digital Product Passport FAQ

Short answers to the questions manufacturers, importers and product teams ask first.

What is a Digital Product Passport?

A DPP is a set of product-specific data accessible electronically through a data carrier, with exact information determined by applicable product rules.

Does every product need a DPP now?

No. ESPR creates the framework, while product-specific delegated acts determine scope and timing. Separate legislation can also create passport obligations.

Is a QR code enough?

No. A QR code can be a data carrier, but DPP also depends on identifiers, required data, access rules and storage.

What is the DPP Registry?

The EU Registry is a central registration layer for DPP identifiers and metadata while passport content follows a decentralised model.

When does the Battery Passport apply?

From 18 February 2027 for LMT batteries, EV batteries and industrial batteries above 2 kWh under Article 77 of the EU Battery Regulation.

What is DPP vs ESPR?

ESPR is the EU regulatory framework; DPP is one mechanism for making product-specific information digitally accessible.
