How the GTIN check works

The tool removes non-numeric characters, checks for a supported GTIN length and recalculates the final check digit using the standard alternating 3/1 weighting method.

Frequently asked questions

What GTIN lengths does this tool validate?

The validator supports GTIN-8, GTIN-12, GTIN-13 and GTIN-14 and checks the final check digit.

Does a valid GTIN prove that the number belongs to my company?

No. A correct check digit only confirms the number is structurally valid. It does not confirm assignment, ownership or product registration.

Is GTIN mandatory for every Digital Product Passport?

No. GTIN may be used where applicable, but product-specific DPP rules and identifier standards determine what is required.
