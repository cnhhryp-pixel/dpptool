Data worth organising now

  • Alloy/grade and product identifiers
  • Primary and recycled material information
  • Producer, facility and supplier records
  • Environmental performance data where required
  • Compliance and technical documentation

Preparation workflow

01

Clean alloy data

Standardise grades, temper, form and product identifiers across sales and production systems.

02

Trace material inputs

Create a repeatable method for associating supplier and recycled-content evidence with a product or batch.

03

Prepare exports

Design CSV/JSON outputs that can be transferred to customer or DPP platforms without manual rewriting.

Why early preparation matters

When a final product-specific measure arrives, the difficult part is often not generating a QR code. It is locating reliable data across suppliers, engineering, compliance, ERP/PIM systems and product documentation. A reusable product-data model reduces that implementation risk.

Frequently asked questions

Does every aluminium product need a DPP today?

Final DPP scope, data fields and application dates depend on the delegated act. Working-plan timing alone is not a legal deadline for every aluminium product.

What should companies prepare now?

Start with clean product identifiers, supplier data ownership, materials/composition, technical and compliance records, and a structure that can be mapped to future product-specific DPP fields.

Is the indicative ESPR timeline a legal deadline?

No. The working-plan timeline is an indicative adoption timeline for measures. The binding obligations and application dates come from the final delegated act or other applicable EU legislation.
