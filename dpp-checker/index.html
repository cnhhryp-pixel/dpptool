How to use this result

This checker is a screening tool, not a declaration of conformity. Under ESPR, product-specific delegated acts determine DPP data, carrier, level (model/batch/item) and access requirements. The Battery Regulation separately sets a battery passport requirement for defined battery categories from 18 February 2027.

Important: do not treat “priority product” as “already universally mandatory”. Final obligations depend on the applicable legal measure.

How the DPP Scope Checker works

The checker combines your selected product group with your role in the supply chain and returns a cautious screening result. It deliberately separates three situations: a concrete passport requirement, an ESPR priority product group, and a product that still needs product-specific review.

Frequently asked questions

What does the DPP Checker tell me?

It provides a high-level screening of the current DPP position for selected product groups and distinguishes confirmed passport obligations from ESPR priority workstreams.

Does a priority product group already have a mandatory DPP?

Not necessarily. Priority status under an ESPR working plan is not the same as a binding application date. Final obligations come from the applicable delegated act or other product legislation.

Can I use the checker as legal advice?

No. It is an informational screening tool and should be followed by a review of the applicable EU legal text and product-specific measure.
