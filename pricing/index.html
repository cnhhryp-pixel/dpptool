Why the paid plans are still roadmap items

DPP product-specific rules and technical standards are still evolving. DPPTool is keeping the currently available functionality free while the hosted-platform layer is designed around portability, supplier workflows and real implementation needs rather than an early checkout page.

What would trigger a paid workflow?

  • Hosting and maintaining large numbers of product passports
  • Bulk product and supplier imports
  • Team roles and restricted-access data
  • API and ERP/PIM integrations
  • Version history and lifecycle updates
  • Implementation support for enterprise data mapping

Free tools remain useful before platform selection

Companies can already use the scope checker, readiness assessment, CSV/JSON validators, QR generator and sector guides to prepare their data foundation without purchasing software.
