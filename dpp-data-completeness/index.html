What the completeness score measures

The tool checks 12 practical data areas that commonly appear in DPP preparation projects. It is not a universal legal field list; instead, it helps identify whether your current product records are mature enough to be mapped into future sector-specific requirements.

How to use the gaps

Identity gaps

Fix unstable product IDs, inconsistent model names and missing manufacturer references first.

Evidence gaps

Connect suppliers, compliance documents and material data to the exact product and version.

Lifecycle gaps

Add repair, durability, end-of-life and version information where relevant to the product group.

Completeness vs compliance

A complete internal product record does not automatically mean legal compliance. The final DPP field set depends on the applicable delegated act or other EU product legislation, and some information may have restricted access rather than being public.
