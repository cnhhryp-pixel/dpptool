China → EU

Digital Product Passport for China exporters.

Chinese factories can reduce buyer friction by preparing clean identifiers, supplier evidence and structured product data before EU customers request it at scale.

What EU buyers may ask Chinese suppliers for

  • Product/model IDs and GTINs where used
  • Manufacturer and facility information
  • Material and component composition
  • Supplier and country-of-origin evidence
  • Declarations, test reports and certificate references
  • Repair, durability, recycled-content or environmental data where the product rules require them

Recommended factory workflow

  1. Create one English product master per SKU/model.
  2. Link each product to materials, components and supplier IDs.
  3. Store compliance evidence with stable document references.
  4. Export the core fields as CSV/JSON instead of sending disconnected spreadsheets.
  5. Let the EU buyer map the structured data into its DPP platform or agree on a shared provider.
