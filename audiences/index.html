Why role matters in a DPP project

The same product can create different operational tasks for different organisations. A manufacturer may own product and engineering data, an EU importer may need to verify upstream evidence, and a brand may coordinate multiple suppliers and customer-facing passport experiences.

Shared foundation across roles

  • Stable product and operator identifiers
  • Reliable product and supplier data
  • Compliance evidence linked to the correct product version
  • Clear ownership for updates and lifecycle maintenance
  • Portable exports that can move between systems and providers

Choose the guide closest to your role

If your organisation performs multiple roles, use more than one guide. For example, a brand that also imports products into the EU may need both supplier-governance and importer-readiness workflows.
