DPP questions companies ask first.

Does every product need a Digital Product Passport now?

No. ESPR creates the framework, while product-specific delegated acts determine when DPP requirements apply to a product group. Separate legislation such as the Battery Regulation can also create passport obligations.

Is a QR code the same as a DPP?

No. The data carrier is the access mechanism. A DPP also needs the required identifier, structured data, access model and storage/continuity architecture defined by applicable rules.

What should exporters prepare first?

Clean identifiers, a reliable product master, supplier/material evidence, compliance documents and machine-readable exports are the most reusable starting points.
