EU importer guide

Digital Product Passport for importers.

Importers need to know whether upstream product data can support DPP registration, customs checks and lifecycle accuracy before goods arrive in the EU.

Importer checklist

  • Confirm the manufacturer and product identifiers are consistent
  • Verify required DPP data can be provided before placing covered products on the market
  • Define who registers and maintains the passport
  • Keep commodity code and customs-facing data aligned
  • Build supplier contracts around structured data, not PDF attachments alone
Imported products matter: the EU Registry supports customs verification for products subject to DPP requirements.
