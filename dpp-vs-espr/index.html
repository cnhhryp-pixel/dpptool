Plain-English comparison

DPP vs ESPR: one is the passport, the other is the framework.

They are closely linked, but they are not interchangeable terms.

DPPESPR
Full nameDigital Product PassportEcodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation
What it isA structured set of product-specific data accessible electronically through a data carrier.EU Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 establishing a framework for ecodesign and information requirements.
What businesses doCreate, maintain, expose and register passport-related data when required.Follow product-specific requirements adopted under the framework.
SEO / buying languageDPP software, DPP generator, DPP compliance, Digital Product PassportESPR requirements, ESPR compliance, ecodesign regulation

Why the distinction matters

A company may need to understand ESPR before it can know whether, when and how a DPP applies. For product teams, however, “DPP” is often the more direct implementation term because it describes the actual passport and supporting software workflow.
