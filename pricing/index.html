Access model: Free is instant. Professional is unlocked on the website with a signed annual Access Key. Enterprise is a custom implementation path.

What changes when you upgrade to Professional?

Professional is not simply “more searches.” It adds a working layer on top of the free checkers: deeper reports, structured exports and higher-volume product analysis. The goal is to help a company move from a one-off compliance question to a repeatable DPP preparation workflow.

How Professional access works without a traditional account backend

  1. Purchase the €49 annual Professional plan through PayPal.
  2. Receive a signed Professional Access Key linked to the payment email.
  3. Open the Professional Access page and enter the email + key.
  4. The browser verifies the digital signature and expiry date.
  5. Professional tools unlock on that browser and the access state is stored locally.
No shared master password: each key is signed, has its own expiry date and is bound to the customer's email. The signing private key is not stored on the website.

Do customers need to email you?

Free: no. Everything is available directly on the website.

Professional: pay €49 through PayPal. After the payment is confirmed, a signed 1-year Access Key is sent to the PayPal payment email. The customer then enters that email + key on the website to unlock Professional. Key issuance is manual in the current version and can later be automated without building a traditional account system.

Enterprise: yes, a direct discussion is appropriate because API, ERP/PIM, supplier and team requirements vary substantially.
