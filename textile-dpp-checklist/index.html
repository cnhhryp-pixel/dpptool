Textile DPP preparation checklist

Textiles, apparel and footwear are priority areas under the ESPR work plan. Use this checklist to prepare reusable product and supplier data before final product-specific rules apply.

Product master

  • Stable style/model identifier
  • SKU, size and colour variant structure
  • GTIN or other identifiers where used
  • Manufacturer and production-site records

Materials and supply chain

  • Fibre and fabric composition
  • Trims and component composition
  • Supplier IDs and country-of-origin evidence
  • Material certificates and supporting documents

Lifecycle information

  • Care instructions
  • Durability and performance data where relevant
  • Repair information
  • Recycling / end-of-life information where required
Do not treat this as the final legal field list. Binding textile DPP fields and dates depend on the applicable delegated act.
