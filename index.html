Digital Product Passport FAQ

Three points worth understanding before starting implementation.

Does every product need a Digital Product Passport now?

No. ESPR creates the framework, while product-specific delegated acts determine when DPP requirements apply to a product group. Separate legislation such as the Battery Regulation can also create passport obligations.

Is a QR code the same as a DPP?

No. A QR code can be a data carrier, but the passport also depends on the correct identifier, structured product data, access rules, hosting and applicable legal requirements.

What should companies prepare first?

Start with stable identifiers, a clean product master, supplier and material evidence, compliance documents, field ownership and machine-readable exports.
