Buyer guide

Digital Product Passport software: what actually matters.

A DPP platform should manage structured product data and long-term access — not just generate a QR code or PDF.

Core capabilities to evaluate

CapabilityWhy it matters
Product data modelMust support the fields and product level required by applicable product-specific rules.
Identifiers & data carriersESPR requires a persistent unique product identifier linked through a data carrier.
Machine-readable dataDPP data should use open, interoperable formats and be structured, searchable and transferable where applicable.
Access controlDifferent actors can have different access rights to passport data.
Back-up & continuityESPR requires a back-up copy through a DPP service provider for products covered by applicable delegated acts.
Bulk import / APICritical when product count grows beyond manual entry.
Supplier workflowMuch of the required data will originate upstream.

Questions to ask vendors

  • Can I export all passport data in a portable machine-readable format?
  • How are identifiers, model/batch/item levels and version history handled?
  • What happens if I stop using the platform?
  • Can suppliers submit data without seeing sensitive fields?
  • Is the platform ready to adapt to product-specific delegated acts?
Avoid lock-in: ESPR explicitly requires open standards and an interoperable exchange network without vendor lock-in for DPP data.
