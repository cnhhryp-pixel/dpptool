Frequently asked questions

Which batteries are covered by the Battery Passport from 18 February 2027?

Article 77 covers LMT batteries, electric vehicle batteries and industrial batteries with capacity greater than 2 kWh, subject to the Regulation's definitions and scope.

Does an industrial battery at exactly 2 kWh fall under the greater-than-2-kWh passport threshold?

The Article 77 threshold is stated as greater than 2 kWh, so an industrial battery at or below 2 kWh is not in that specific threshold category, though other Battery Regulation obligations may still apply.

Does this checker cover every Battery Regulation requirement?

No. It only screens the passport category and threshold. Other battery obligations and data requirements must be reviewed separately.
