Battery Passport

Battery Passport preparation for manufacturers.

For covered LMT, EV and industrial batteries above 2 kWh, the EU Battery Passport has a concrete 18 February 2027 date.

Manufacturing-side preparation

  • Confirm the legal battery category and capacity
  • Map model-level and individual-battery data
  • Assign stable identifiers and data carriers
  • Connect manufacturing and lifecycle records
  • Define update responsibility for information that changes during use
