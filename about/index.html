About

Practical DPP tools built around verifiable requirements.

DPPTool is designed to help companies structure product data, screen DPP scope and prepare for EU Digital Product Passport requirements without turning regulatory uncertainty into false deadlines.

Our approach

We separate confirmed legal requirements from working-plan priorities and proposals. Core legal statements link back to primary EU sources wherever practical.

What DPPTool is not

DPPTool is not a law firm, notified body, market-surveillance authority or EU institution. The tools are informational and do not constitute legal advice or certification.

Product roadmap

  • Free product-scope and readiness tools
  • Structured DPP data generation
  • QR / identifier validation
  • Sector templates and supplier-data workflows
  • Machine-readable exports and API-ready schemas
