Textiles & apparel

DPP preparation for textile manufacturers.

Textile and apparel businesses should start structuring fibre, material, supplier, durability and care data while product-specific ESPR requirements continue to develop.

Factory data to organise now

  • Fabric and fibre composition
  • Trims and component suppliers
  • Factory and country-of-origin records
  • Care, durability and repair information
  • Chemical/substance and compliance evidence where applicable
  • Consistent model, size and colour identifiers
Timing: textiles are an ESPR priority product group, but final legal scope and application dates come from the applicable delegated act.
