Digital Product Passport implementation checklist

Use this checklist to move from regulatory research to an operational DPP project without starting with software procurement too early.

1. Confirm legal scope

  • Identify the exact product group and EU legislation
  • Confirm whether a binding DPP requirement exists or is still in a working-plan stage
  • Identify the economic operator responsible for placing the product on the EU market

2. Build the product-data foundation

  • Clean product/model identifiers
  • Standardise supplier and facility identifiers
  • Link materials, components and compliance evidence
  • Assign an owner to each data field

3. Design the passport architecture

  • Define model, batch or item level as required
  • Select the data carrier and resolver approach
  • Separate public and restricted information
  • Plan hosting, backup and portability

4. Prepare operations

  • Map Registry registration responsibilities
  • Test CSV/API exports
  • Set version-control and update workflows
  • Document how data remains available through the required lifecycle
