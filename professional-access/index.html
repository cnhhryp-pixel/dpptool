What happens after you pay?

  1. Complete the €49 PayPal payment.
  2. The payment is confirmed using the PayPal transaction record.
  3. A signed 1-year Professional Access Key is generated for the PayPal payment email.
  4. The key is sent to that email address.
  5. Return to this page, enter the email + key, and Professional unlocks.
Not instant yet: key issuance is manual in this first version, so payment and access-key delivery are separate steps. The payment flow can be automated later with a small webhook/serverless function.
